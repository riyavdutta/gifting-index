Six houses, chosen by test

I tested nine more houses before choosing these six. Old heritage houses like Penhaligon’s, Acqua di Parma and Floris spike like cult houses, so “classic” here means the name people reach for as a present, not the oldest. Molton Brown and Frédéric Malle joined because their patterns are clean and ten years long.

Medians, not averages

Each month or week is compared with that house’s own typical month or week, then the median is taken across years. A first pass found a March bump for Jo Malone; medians showed it was a few one-off spikes, so it was cut. One loud week can’t make a pattern here.

Ten moments, honestly dated

Moon-based holidays (Lunar New Year, Eid, Raksha Bandhan, Diwali) can shift by a day. Google is blocked in mainland China, so Lunar New Year and Singles’ Day are under-counted. Diwali sits at the start of the winter run-up, so part of its lift is the season itself. The year flower is the typical year across 2022–2025.

The data writes the headline

Every number on this page is calculated in your browser from the latest data, fetched every morning. If a cult house out-gifts the classics this year, the page will say so.
