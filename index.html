Each pair is one December. The plum stem is Jo Malone, Diptyque and Molton Brown; the green one is Le Labo, Byredo and Frédéric Malle.

One house, the whole year.

Each blossom is a month, and its size is how much people search that month against a usual one.

Things I'm still not sure about.

Google is blocked in mainland China, so Lunar New Year and Singles' Day are probably bigger than they look here.

Before settling on these six, I tested nine other houses. Penhaligon's and Acqua di Parma behave like the niche houses, so "classic" here means the name people reach for as a present, not the oldest.

I compare each house with its own usual month and take the middle value across the years, so one strange week can't make a pattern.

Every number is worked out again each morning. If a niche house out-gifts the classics one year, this page will show it.
